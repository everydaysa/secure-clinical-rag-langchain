# POL-IMG-004 — Advanced Imaging for Low Back Pain

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Invented for a demo application. Not real coverage
> policy and not medical advice.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers MRI and CT of the lumbar spine for members aged 18 and over
presenting with low back pain. It does not cover imaging ordered in the
emergency department or during an inpatient stay.

## 2. Medical necessity criteria

Advanced imaging is considered medically necessary when **either** condition
is met:

1. The member has completed at least **6 weeks of conservative therapy**
   (physical therapy, NSAIDs, or a structured activity programme) documented in
   the medical record, and symptoms persist or have worsened; or
2. One or more **red flag findings** are documented, in which case the 6-week
   requirement is waived:
   - progressive neurological deficit
   - suspected cauda equina syndrome
   - history of malignancy with new back pain
   - suspected spinal infection or epidural abscess
   - significant trauma, or minor trauma in a member with osteoporosis

## 3. Exclusions

- Repeat imaging within 6 months of a prior study of the same region without a
  documented change in clinical status.
- Imaging performed solely to satisfy a member request.

## 4. Documentation requirements

Requests must include the ordering clinician's notes covering duration of
symptoms, conservative therapy attempted with dates, neurological findings, and
the clinical question the study is expected to answer.

## 5. Turnaround times

Standard determinations are issued within 5 business days of a complete
request. Expedited requests are decided within 72 hours.
