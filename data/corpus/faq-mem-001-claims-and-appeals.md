## How long does a claim take?

Clean electronic claims are processed within **15 business days**. Paper claims
take up to 30 business days. A claim is "clean" when it has a valid member ID,
provider identifier, diagnosis and procedure codes, and date of service.

## Why was my claim denied?

The most common reasons are a missing prior authorization, a service received
out of network, a benefit limit already met for the plan year, or a coding
mismatch between the diagnosis and the procedure. The denial letter names the
specific reason and the policy it came from.

## How do I appeal?

1. File within **60 calendar days** of the denial letter.
2. Submit online, by mail, or by phone. Include the claim number and any
   supporting records from your provider.
3. Standard appeals are decided within **30 calendar days**. If waiting could
   seriously harm your health, ask for an **expedited appeal**, decided within
   **72 hours**.
4. If the appeal is denied, you may request an **external review** by an
   independent organisation within 4 months.

## Can someone appeal on my behalf?

Yes. Your provider or an authorised representative may appeal for you. An
authorised representative form must be on file first.

## Where do I check status?

Sign in to the member portal, or call the number on your member ID card.
