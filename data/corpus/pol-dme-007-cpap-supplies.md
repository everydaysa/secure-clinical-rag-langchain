# POL-DME-007 — CPAP Devices and Replacement Supplies

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Invented for a demo application. Not real coverage
> policy and not medical advice.

## 1. Initial device

A CPAP device is covered following a diagnosis of obstructive sleep apnea
confirmed by an attended sleep study or an approved home sleep apnea test, with
a documented apnea-hypopnea index of 15 or more, or 5 or more accompanied by
documented symptoms such as excessive daytime sleepiness.

## 2. Adherence requirement

Continued coverage after the initial 90 days requires documented adherence:
use on at least **21 of 30 consecutive nights**, at least 4 hours per night,
evidenced by device download data, plus a clinical re-evaluation confirming
benefit.

## 3. Replacement supply schedule

| Item | Maximum frequency |
|---|---|
| Mask | 1 every 3 months |
| Cushions or pillows | 2 per month |
| Tubing | 1 every 3 months |
| Disposable filters | 2 per month |
| Humidifier chamber | 1 every 6 months |

Quantities above the schedule require documentation of loss, damage, or a
clinical change such as significant weight change requiring a new mask size.

## 4. Rental to purchase

Devices are rented for 13 months of continuous use, after which ownership
transfers to the member. Rental payments stop at transfer.
