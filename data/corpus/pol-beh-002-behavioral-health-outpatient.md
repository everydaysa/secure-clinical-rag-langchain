# POL-BEH-002 — Outpatient Behavioral Health Services

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Invented for a demo application. Not real coverage
> policy and not medical advice.
>
> RESTRICTED: this document is limited to clinical reviewers and compliance.
> It is deliberately excluded from general claims and member-services access to
> demonstrate role-based retrieval.

## 1. Scope

Outpatient behavioral health services delivered by licensed practitioners,
including individual and group therapy, medication management, and intensive
outpatient programmes.

## 2. Authorization

- Individual outpatient therapy: **no prior authorization** for the first 20
  visits per plan year.
- Intensive outpatient programmes: prior authorization required, reviewed every
  10 sessions against continued-stay criteria.
- Medication management visits: no prior authorization.

## 3. Continued stay review

Continued participation requires documented treatment goals, measurable
progress or a documented change of plan where progress has stalled, and an
active discharge plan.

## 4. Confidentiality handling

Records under this policy carry heightened confidentiality handling. Case notes
are stored separately from general claims history, and access is logged. Any
downstream system that surfaces this content must record who accessed it and
under which role.

## 5. Parity

Utilization management applied under this policy must be no more restrictive
than that applied to comparable medical or surgical benefits.
