# GUIDE-CARD-001 — Hypertension Management Guideline

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Invented for a demo application. Not real clinical
> guidance and not medical advice.

## 1. Diagnosis

A diagnosis of hypertension requires elevated readings on **two separate
occasions**, measured with a validated device after the member has been seated
quietly for five minutes. Where readings are borderline, ambulatory or home
monitoring over seven days is preferred to a third office reading.

## 2. Treatment targets

- General adult population: below 130/80 mm Hg.
- Members aged 75 and over: individualised targets, weighing fall risk and
  overall burden of treatment against expected benefit.

## 3. Follow-up intervals

| Situation | Review interval |
|---|---|
| Newly started therapy | 4 weeks |
| Target achieved, stable | 3 to 6 months |
| Target not achieved | 4 weeks after each adjustment |

## 4. Care management referral

Refer to the care management programme when a member has uncontrolled
hypertension after two medication adjustments, repeated missed appointments, or
documented barriers to filling prescriptions.

## 5. Quality measures

This guideline supports the plan's blood pressure control quality measure.
Documentation of the most recent reading is required for measure credit.
