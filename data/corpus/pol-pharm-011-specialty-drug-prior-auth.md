# POL-PHARM-011 — Specialty Drug Prior Authorization

> SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Invented for a demo application. Not real coverage
> policy and not medical advice.

## 1. Scope

Applies to all drugs on the plan's specialty tier, including biologics and
injectable therapies dispensed through the specialty pharmacy network.

## 2. General requirements

All specialty drug requests require:

1. A diagnosis consistent with the drug's approved indication.
2. Documentation of **step therapy**: trial and failure, intolerance, or
   contraindication for at least one preferred alternative, unless the member
   is continuing therapy started under a prior plan within the last 12 months.
3. A prescriber attestation that the member is being monitored for the adverse
   effects listed in the product labelling.

## 3. Continuity of care

Members new to the plan who are stabilised on a specialty drug receive a
**90-day transition fill** while the authorization is reviewed. Coverage is not
interrupted during this window.

## 4. Authorization periods

- Initial authorization: 6 months.
- Renewal: 12 months, with documented clinical response.
- Renewal requests submitted without response documentation are returned as
  incomplete rather than denied.

## 5. Appeals

Adverse determinations may be appealed within 60 calendar days. Expedited
appeals are decided within 72 hours where a delay would jeopardise the member's
health. See FAQ-MEM-001 for the member-facing summary.
